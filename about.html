What is She&Strength?

She&Strength is a gym where women can enjoy their workouts without feeling uncomfortable around men. With state-of-the-art equipment, we provide a high-quality, women-only environment.

Why focus on a safe space for women?

We want women to focus on their physical and mental health without having to think about feeling insecure or uncomfortable. We don’t just offer a gym — we offer a safe space.

How to sign up?

We use a tier system so you can choose which facilities you want access to. Our gym has four main sections, including the main hall, an MMA studio, a swimming pool, and a training court.
