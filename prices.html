Membership Tiers

Note: Each tier determines which facilities can be accessed. Please refer to the About page.

Tier 1 — Standard / Strongwoman Tier — £19.99 per month

Access to the main hall for the price of £19.99 per month

Tier 2 — Supergirl Tier — £29.99 per month

Access to one of the selected specialised areas for the price of £29.99 per month. The specialised areas include an MMA studio, a swimming pool, and a training court.

Tier 3 — Superwoman Tier — £39.99 per month

Access to two of the selected specialised areas for the price of £39.99 per month. The specialised areas include an MMA studio, a swimming pool, and a training court.

Tier 4 — Wonder Woman Tier — £49.99 per month

Full access to all gym facilities, including specialised areas. The specialised areas include an MMA studio, a swimming pool, and a training court.
